I am a PhD student at Harbin Institute of Technology, Shenzhen. My research focuses on machine learning for life sciences, particularly protein language models, function prediction, and knowledge-guided learning. I also explore retrieval-augmented prediction and scientific agent workflows.

Please feel free to reach out at yaxuanwy@gmail.com for research discussions and potential collaborations.

Research Interests

  • Protein representation learning. Protein language models, domain segmentation, and domain-level multi-label function prediction.
  • Knowledge-guided learning. Language models and graph neural networks for commonsense reasoning and biomedical knowledge modeling.
  • Scientific workflows and multimodal learning. Retrieval-augmented prediction, scientific agent workflows, and cross-modal representation learning.

Manuscripts & Ongoing Research

  • A Study on Cross-Species Transmission Risk of Betacoronaviruses Based on RBD–Mammalian Receptor Interactions and Machine Learning

    Collaborative research

    Research in progress

    A collaborative computational study of viral–host receptor interactions in the context of cross-species transmission risk. The work uses protein representation learning and machine learning to support interaction analysis and research evaluation.

  • Cross-Species Transmission Risk of Coronaviruses Based on Spike RBD and Host Receptor Interactions

    Co-first author (third-listed)

    Manuscript in preparation

    An ongoing collaborative study with a focus on scientific workflow development. The work connects data processing, model training, prediction, and result review through agent-based orchestration, with language models supporting research summaries and reporting.

  • QuaSF Protein Domain Segmentation and Function Prediction with Disentangling Queries

    First author

    BioinformaticsSubmitted

    A sequence-based framework for protein domain segmentation and domain-level multi-label function prediction. QuaSF disentangles segmentation queries and function queries, combining protein language model representations with regional features and inter-domain context to connect domain localization with functional understanding.

  • Cross-Modal Mutual Transfer Quantization for Generative Recommendation

    Third author

    ACM The Web Conference 2027Submitted

    Collaborative work on cross-modal semantic interaction in discrete item representations for generative recommendation. MUTRARec brings cross-modal feature fusion into quantization, using shared representations and lightweight modality adapters to capture both common and modality-specific information.
